    How the Skill Gap Analyzer Works
    1. NLP Text & Skill Extraction

    Extracts tokenized text from PDF, DOCX, or pasted text. Uses multi-word n-gram normalization to detect technical skills, frameworks, tools, and contact info.

    2. SQLite & Pandas Gap Calculation

    Maps detected competencies against the job roles and required skills taxonomy stored in SQLite database. Weighs must-have core skills (2x) against secondary skills (1x).

    3. ATS Hygiene & Verbs Scorer

    Evaluates section architecture, action verb strength, and quantifiable numbers to predict how well resume screeners parse your file.

    4. Dynamic Roadmap & Quizzes

    Generates a tailored learning timeline with curated courses, self-diagnostic quizzes, and mock interview questions.
